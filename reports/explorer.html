BEYOND THE PRICE TAG

Performance profiles

Players are grouped by five performance indicators. Market prices do not determine these clusters, and profile numbers are descriptive labels, not rankings.

How to read the profiles

KMeans groups players by minutes played, goals per 90, assists per 90, yellow cards per 90 and minutes per appearance. The groups overlap; defensive and goalkeeper quality cannot be measured well with these limited statistics.

THE TRANSFER MARKET

A valuation is only part of the deal.

Actual paid transfer fees compared with strictly preceding market estimates. Contract length, negotiations and other unobserved factors also influence the final price.

About this separate analysis

Paid transactions from July–September 2025. Free transfers and undisclosed or nonpositive fees are excluded. A Transfermarkt estimate and a realized transfer fee are different variables.
